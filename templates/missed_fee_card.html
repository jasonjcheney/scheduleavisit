Missed first-visit fee

Optional. When this is on, a client booking a first visit or intake saves a card and is not charged today. If you mark a no-show, or they cancel inside the window, you can charge that card the amount below once. The money is paid to you through Stripe. This is not insurance or a copay, and it is not a charge for the visit itself.
