How it works

Three steps — and you still leave with a time.

1

Search a name or a city

No portal login. No phone tree. Look up the person you came to see, or a place you can get to.

2

Pick a day and a time

Choose a free consult or a full session when they offer it. Leave your name. That is the whole form.

3

If they are full, you still get a time

You stay on the page and see a colleague they trust. If that person is full too, the next trusted colleague is offered.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to book?

No. Search, pick a time, and leave your name. If your clinician is full, you still see a trusted colleague.

What happens when a clinician is full?

Trusted peers on their network get offered. If everyone is full, you can join a waitlist.

Do clients see the hour cap?

No. They see open times or a colleague the clinician trusts.

Is this HIPAA?

Not yet. No BAA. Do not put clinical notes here. Fine for scheduling while you grow.

How do I get a booking link?

Sign up or log in. Your public link and QR are on the dashboard.
