FAQ

Common questions

Short answers for therapists and the people who book with them.

What happens when I’m full?

When you hit your weekly hour cap, clients who open your link still leave with a time — with a trusted peer you invited. If that peer is full too, we walk your network until someone has room. If everyone reachable is booked, they can leave a waitlist note you see on your dashboard (not email yet).

Do clients see my hour cap?

No. Clients never see your weekly hour number. They only see open times with you, or a colleague you recommend when you’re at capacity.

How do peer referrals work?

From your dashboard, invite colleagues by email. When they accept, you become mutual trusted peers. Overflow can hop one or more steps through that network so the client still leaves with a name and a time.

Is this HIPAA?

No. ScheduleAVisit is not a HIPAA product. It helps with booking and capacity — not clinical notes, payments, or insurance billing.

How do I get my booking link?

Sign up, finish setup, and you’ll get a public link like scheduleavisit.com/p/your-name. Copy it (or the QR) from your dashboard and share it wherever clients find you.
