FAQ

Common questions

Plain answers for people booking a visit — and for clinicians who want a booking link.

Do I need an account to book?

No. Open a booking link, pick a time, and leave your name. If your clinician is full, you still see a trusted colleague.

What happens when I’m full?

Trusted peers on your network get offered; if everyone is full, clients can join your waitlist.

Do clients see my hour cap?

No. They see open times or a colleague you trust.

How do peer referrals work?

You invite colleagues; when you’re at capacity the booking page offers them (including wider network hops).

Is this HIPAA?

Not yet. No BAA. Don’t put clinical notes here. Fine for scheduling logistics while you grow.

How do I get my booking link?

Sign up / log in → setup → dashboard shows your public link and QR. Notifications are in-dashboard for now (no email yet).
