## What shipped overnight

A full night of product polish. In plain terms:

- **Multi-hop referral** — when someone is full, they can pass a visitor to a peer (and that peer can pass again) without dead ends.
- **Waitlist** — visitors can hold a place when there’s no open slot yet.
- **Consult vs session** — clear distinction so people know what they’re booking.
- **Calendar** — setup and booking calendar flow for providers.
- **Portal links** — shareable booking pages for each practice.
- **.ics calendar files** — add-the-visit-to-your-phone/calendar downloads.
- **QR codes** — easy share for print or in-person.
- **Password change** — providers can update their own login.
- **Reschedule** — move a visit without starting over.
- **Client filter** — find the right person in the list faster.
- **In-app notifications** — alerts inside the product (email not wired yet).
- **FAQ** — honest, plain-language answers on the landing page.
- **Honesty footer** — sitewide note that this is scheduling-only for now.
- **Design passes** — calmer empty states, branded not-found pages, and visual polish.
